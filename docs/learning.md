# Learning

What to show before the room deploys.

## Intro

[How to build an AI brain for your business in a weekend (Dorsey’s method)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=grpWNr71O5w). Upgraded, 16:37, 18 May 2026.

Play the opening, and stop when the install steps start, at about 4:40. That stretch is the problem, Karpathy’s personal knowledge base, Dorsey’s company version, and the picture of sources, a wiki the model maintains, and a schema. The rest of the video is a different build: Obsidian, Drive for desktop, Slack export, and a weekly Claude Code routine.

Use these points, then go to the slides:

- The files already exist. Docs, mail, transcripts, the CRM, invoices. One file does not show the pattern across them.
- A small company can do this. It has about a dozen sources, not a thousand.
- The model should maintain the record. The person reads it. New material arrives as a file, and the model writes the update.
- The schema is the instruction sheet, so the next run does not start from nothing.

In this room the schema is `ontology.yaml`. The record is the chunks and the graph in Postgres. The person leaves with that running on their own Render box, and they ask Claude a real question before they go. They do not leave with an Obsidian vault.

The prompts from the video stay on the creator’s page: [Video Resources — AI Brain for Your Business](https://www.notion.so/benstevenson/Video-Resources-AI-Brain-for-Your-Business-361be0f4c24f8143b520d5dc4978efeb).

## Say this

About 30 seconds. The first two sentences are the idea from that opening. The rest is this day.

Every business already has the files. Docs, email, invoices. Finding one fact is hard. Seeing the pattern across all of them is harder.

Saturday 5 December. One day. We build a company brain.

You leave with it running. The server stays in your name. You connect Claude and ask it a real question.

Cohort 1. 450 dollars. Bring your laptop.

Cut the room under the date, the laptop under the server line, you presenting under Claude, and you with a guest on the price.
